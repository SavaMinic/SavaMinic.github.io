[Global Game Jam 2026](https://globalgamejam.org/) finished with 474,517+ Jammers who created 101,246+ Games in 132 Countries all over the world 🌎 <br />

Our [Game Dev Guild location in Belgrade](https://globalgamejam.org/jam-sites/2026/global-game-jam-belgrage-2026) participated with 128 jammers who created 29 games - and we are thrilled to be a part of this amazing initiative, with the help of our friends from [Playstudios Europe](https://www.instagram.com/playstudioseurope/), [Wargaming Belgrade](https://www.instagram.com/wargaming/) and [Stikeraj](https://www.instagram.com/stikeraj.rs/) 💚 <br />

Being part of a global initiative while strengthening a local community is where the real impact happens ✨ <br />

Check out the [Gameplay videos](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUwnzoFckRX10q_RMwpzeScXGr9yeY6PF) and [the full presentation stream](https://youtu.be/LjB0cISJwB0).<br />
